# Code of conduct

Sona is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to ask, report and build. Everyone who takes part in
its issues, pull requests and discussions agrees to this.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Many people here are new to Swift, audio or open source.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Say what you'd change and why.
- Accept a "no" on a change gracefully: Sona stays small on purpose (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Not OK

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Unwanted sexual attention, or comments about someone's body, identity or background.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Posting someone else's private information, recordings or transcripts without their clear permission. Sona records
  speech, so this one matters here: never attach audio or notes of real people to an issue.
- Spam, and repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this privately, through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/dinilb/sona/security/advisories/new)
on this repository. Reports are kept private. Security and privacy bugs in the app go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, or block someone from the project,
temporarily or for good, depending on what happened. You'll be told why when that's safe to do.

This is a short version in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is the guide for
anything it doesn't cover.
